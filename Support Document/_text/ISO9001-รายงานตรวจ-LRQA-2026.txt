01. Executive report
Based on the audit outcome the Audit Team recommends the ISO 9001:2015 certification of Mitr Lao Sugar Co.,
Ltd. for the agreed scope.
The management system was audited remotely and found to be implemented effectively, meeting ISO9001:2015
and stakeholder requirements. Certification is recommended to continue, with evidence confirming closure of
previous issues and robust compliance controls.
The Audit Team Leader confirms the contractual arrangements for ISO 9001:2015 require amendment and that the
next visit may not proceed before the necessary amendments are made in the contract. Details are recorded in this
report.
Continual improvement:
There is clear evidence of ongoing continual improvement, particularly in KPI monitoring, supplier evaluation,
inventory accuracy, preventive maintenance, and calibration control. The system remains effective in delivering
desired outcomes, though certain KPIs in procurement satisfaction and maintenance results are being targeted for
corrective action.

and timescales

During the organic sugar production period, a total of 836 tons of

preventing the required purity drop and negatively impacting
crystallization efficiency.

2) determining the causes of the nonconformity; 3) determining if
similar nonconformities exist, or could potentially occur; c)

corrective action taken; e) update risks and opportunities determined
