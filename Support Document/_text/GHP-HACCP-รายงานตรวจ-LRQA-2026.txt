Statement of non conformity Partial Insufficient of lot identification.
Evidence Lot identification of DCR 1 kg was defined MFG date, strike number,
and shift packing of jumbo bag, but it was not sufficient to know
repacking date in case many repacking dates and may not know lot
of packaging.
Proposed correction, corrective action
and timescales
Root cause
The organization’s traceability system design did not define the
repacking process as a new lot-creation point, and no traceability
risk assessment was conducted for scenarios involving multiple
repacking activities. As a result, the lot identification system was not
able to clearly identify the repacking date or fully link the relevant
packaging lots in accordance with ISO 22000 clause 8.3
Correction
The organization will record the repacking date and the packaging
lot information on the packaging containers for DCR 1 kg products
that are currently in process and in inventory, in order to ensure full
traceability of the repacking lots.
Corrective action
• The organization will improve the lot identification method for
products subject to repacking by incorporating the repacking date
and the relevant packaging lot information into the lot identification
system.
• The organization will revise the repacking WI/SOP to clearly define
the procedures for identifying and recording lot identification
information.
•The organization will communicate and provide training to relevant
personnel involved in the repacking process to ensure compliance
with the revised lot identification system.
•The organization will establish verification activities for lot
identification records after repacking to confirm that the traceability
system is fully effective.
Correction
Root cause analysis
Requirement 14.1 Lot identification and traceability
Lot identification or other identification strategies are essential in
product recall and also help effective stock rotation. Each container
of food should be permanently marked to identify the producer and
the lot. The General Standard for the Labelling of Pre-packaged
Foods (CXS 1-1985)7 applies.

The generic audit objectives and team responsibilities are included in the Client Information Note ‘Assessment Process’. The
assessment standard and roles of the audit team are defined in the assessment visit confirmation to the client by LRQA. Any
revised scope will be as agreed in formal correspondence between LRQA and the client or defined in this section of the previous
LRQA visit report. Where identified above see separate assessment plan (latest issue) for further detail. Any additional observers

management system processes and documentation.

Sampling
The assessment process relies on taking a sample of the activities of the business. This is not statistically based but uses
representative examples. Not all of the detailed nature of a business may be sampled so, if no issues are raised in a particular
process, it does not necessarily mean that there are no issues, and if issues are raised, it does not necessarily mean that these are
the only issues.
